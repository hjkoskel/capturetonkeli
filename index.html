Wi-Fi monitor mode for your browser: an ESP32-C5 USB dongle captures 802.11 frames on 2.4 and 5 GHz, filters them on the device and streams them here. It only receives; it never transmits.

Opens in its own window, works offline, starts from the home screen or app menu.

(factory-fresh XIAO ESP32-C5 or other firmware)

Desktop: Chrome, Edge, Opera, or Firefox ≥ 151 (Web Serial). Android: Chrome (USB-OTG; WebUSB, or Web Serial where Chrome offers USB ports). iPhone/iPad are not supported.
